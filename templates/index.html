04 // SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & TRAINING MATRIX

CONSTRUCTIVIST PIPELINE
01

SYNTHETIC DEGRADATION

Clean color photographs are converted to Lab color space. Luminance ($L$) is extracted and subjected to random Bezier scratch vectors, film grain noise, and Gaussian defocus blur to create paired training data.

02

U-NET GENERATOR

An 8-stage encoder-decoder network with skip connections directly transfers high-frequency spatial boundaries across the bottleneck, simultaneously inpainting missing scratches and inferring natural chrominance.

03

70×70 PATCHGAN

Instead of evaluating global scalars, the PatchGAN discriminator classifies each local $70\times 70$ image tile as real or fake, penalizing blurry averages and enforcing razor-sharp edge realism.

04

ADVERSARIAL + L1 LOSS

The combined loss objective ($\mathcal{L}_{cGAN} + 100 \cdot \mathcal{L}_{L1}$) drives convergence. The L1 loss preserves structural fidelity to the scene while the adversarial loss enforces vivid natural color palettes.
